# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4, Motion, Lenis. Pinned by the brief. (Inferred from the brief; no interview round was held.)

## Users
Commodity producers, traders, importers, exporters, steel mills, power companies and industrial end-users who need dry bulk tonnage and who judge a chartering house on trust, clarity and execution.

## Product Purpose
One-page marketing site for Hilf Shipping, a dry bulk ship operator based in Dubai, UAE. Success: a charterer or cargo owner reaches the chartering desk (form or email) with cargo details.

## Positioning
Dry bulk chartering with ethical global execution: voyage charter, time charter and commercial management, run by a named pre-fixture, post-fixture and finance team.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Single page, anchors #home #about #business #why-us #teams #clients #cargo #contact. All copy lives in lib/content.ts and is used verbatim. No invented statistics. "Ahmed Athif" appears twice in the team list on the live site; kept as-is pending client confirmation.

## Brand Commitments
Logo: navy "hilf" wordmark with grey Arabic calligraphy (/public/images/hilp-shipping-logo.jpeg). Tagline: "Your Voyage, Our Expertise". Navy replaces black everywhere.

## Evidence on Hand
Client logos (8), cargo imagery, hero video, team names and titles, Business Bay office address. Assets are supplied manually by the owner; none were present at build time. Social URLs, the real map embed URL and the form endpoint are not yet supplied.

## Product Principles
1. Trustworthy and precise over loud.
2. Content is sacred: nothing shortened, nothing invented.
3. Quiet-confident: calm, slow, never bouncy.
4. Anti-reference: busy stock-photo corporate shipping templates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, reduced-motion respected, all content in the DOM without JS, video pausable.
